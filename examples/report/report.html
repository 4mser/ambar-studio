01 · Overview

One repository for every format.

AMBAR treats video, slides and documents as the same thing: a page rendered by Chrome. The difference is only how it leaves the browser.

VIDEO

render(t)

Frames stepped one by one and encoded with ffmpeg, with narration and a mix.

SLIDES

.slide

A presenter in the browser and a PDF with one page per slide.

DOCUMENTS

.page

A4 pages with edge-to-edge color, exported exactly as designed.

Commands

CommandOutput
ambar studio <project>Timeline studio in the browser
ambar render <project>MP4 with narration, music and effects
ambar slides deck.htmlPDF plus a PNG of every slide
ambar doc report.htmlA4 PDF
